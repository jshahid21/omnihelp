# EcoHome Smart Thermostat User Guide

**Model:** EcoHome TH-400  
**Warranty:** 2-year limited warranty from date of purchase.

## Setup

**Wi-Fi Connection:** To connect to Wi-Fi, hold the menu button for 5 seconds until the Wi-Fi icon blinks. Open the EcoHome app on your phone, select "Add Device," and choose your 2.4 GHz home network. The thermostat does not support 5 GHz networks.

**Initial Temperature Calibration:** On first boot, the thermostat runs a 10-minute calibration cycle. Do not adjust the temperature during this period or calibration will restart.

**Compatible HVAC Systems:** The TH-400 supports single-stage and two-stage heating and cooling systems. It is not compatible with electric baseboard heaters or steam radiator systems.

## Troubleshooting

**Blank Screen:** If the screen goes blank, replace the two AA batteries in the back panel. Remove the thermostat from the wall mount by pressing the release tab at the bottom, then slide the battery compartment open.

**Error Code E-72:** Error Code E-72 means the HVAC wire (C-wire) is disconnected or faulty. Turn off your HVAC system at the breaker, then check that the C-wire is firmly seated in the terminal labeled "C" on the back of the thermostat.

**Error Code E-14:** Error Code E-14 indicates a Wi-Fi authentication failure. Delete the device from the EcoHome app and re-run the Wi-Fi setup procedure.

**Thermostat Not Reaching Set Temperature:** If the room temperature does not reach the set point within 45 minutes, check that the HVAC air filter is clean. A clogged filter is the most common cause of poor heating or cooling performance.

## Maintenance

- Replace batteries every 12 months even if the low-battery indicator has not appeared.
- Clean the thermostat face with a dry, lint-free cloth only. Do not use water or cleaning sprays.
- Check for firmware updates monthly in the EcoHome app under Settings → Device Info → Check for Update.

All EcoHome thermostats are covered by our standard 30-day return policy. Warranty claims for defects can be submitted at support.ecohome.example.com within the 2-year warranty period.
